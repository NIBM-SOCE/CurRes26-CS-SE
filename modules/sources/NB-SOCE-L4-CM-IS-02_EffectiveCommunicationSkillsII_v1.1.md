## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To take a student who can communicate competently and make them effective inside an organisation. The module works on four fronts: the written forms that professional work actually produces, the performance settings that decide careers, the interpersonal conduct that makes someone workable with, and the habit of looking honestly at one's own practice.

Its scheduling is deliberate. The module sits before the Industrial Training placement block, so the interview strand is preparation rather than retrospect, and the reporting strand is rehearsed before it is required in the workplace.

The reflective strand is not decoration. Assertiveness without reflection produces the wrong graduate, and the two pathway records that named these strands were right to hold them together.

### Specification Boundary (quoted from 01_Shared_Spine.md v2.3, entry IS.2 - QUOTED, never edited)

> **Covers:** advanced professional communication in workplace and organisational settings; technical and business reporting and documentation; interview and professional-dialogue performance; interpersonal communication, assertiveness and reflective practice.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** academic-writing and research-readiness preparation (Capstone Project, on every pathway); the interpersonal-theory and emotional-intelligence depth of NB5006CEM; entrepreneurship content (CREST); pathway technical content.
>
> *Boundary changes are change requests against Master Decisions, not descriptor edits.*

## 4. Skills Programme and Delivery

### Workshop Programme

| Ref | Theme | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Workplace communication: organisational structures, meetings and written channels | 3 |
| W2 | Technical and business reporting and documentation | 3 |
| W3 | Persuasive communication: proposals and pitches | 3 |
| W4 | Interview and professional-dialogue performance | 3 |
| W5 | Interpersonal communication and assertiveness, with structured reflection on personal practice | 3 |
|  | Total workshop hours | 15 |

Workshops run as five three-hour blocks (V10 session pattern). Facilitated activities (21 hours) include report and proposal studios and mock-interview practice; coaching (9 hours) gives individual feedback ahead of the assessed dialogue and supports the reflective strand; independent work (55 hours) completes the portfolio. Scheduling places the module before the Industrial Training placement block.

**Adoption record for this programme.** Against the prior v1.0 structure, the professional-presence workshop is absorbed into W5, which now carries interpersonal communication, assertiveness and reflection together. Total workshop hours are unchanged at 15. The cost is named: presence and networking receive less dedicated time than in v1.0, which is the price of restoring two strands that two pathway records require and that the module previously did not deliver at all.
