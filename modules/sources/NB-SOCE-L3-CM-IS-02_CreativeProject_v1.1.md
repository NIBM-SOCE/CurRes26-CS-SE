## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To give every first-year student the experience of taking a creative computing idea from conception to a demonstrated artefact, with structure: scoping, planning, iterative building, public demonstration and reflection.

The module is one of the two designed vehicles for the public project portfolio, and the first place teamwork is formally practised. That is why its participation mode is fixed rather than left to local judgment. A team build teaches what individual work cannot, and an individually graded reflective component secures the attribution that portfolio evidence depends on. Employers and accrediting bodies both ask what a specific student did. The module is built so the answer exists.

Project themes are free and typically anchor in the team's pathway interests. Pathway-specific project context and briefs may be set beneath the adopted boundary; the participation mode and the individually graded component may not be varied.

### Specification Boundary (quoted from 01_Shared_Spine.md v2.3, entry IS.3 - QUOTED, never edited)

> **Covers:** a facilitated Year 1 project integrating Year 1 learning; ideation, scoping and planning; supervised build and iteration; presentation and demonstration of outcomes; project ownership and structured reflection.
>
> **Participation mode (canonical, not descriptor-variable):** team-based build with an individually graded reflective component. Individual attribution of the reflective component is a condition of the module's use as portfolio evidence.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** pathway-specific applied capstone depth (Capstone Project); degree-level independent research (NB6020CEM).
>
> *Boundary changes are change requests against Master Decisions, not descriptor edits.*

**LO provenance note (canonical device):** the set is authored from the adopted common text at 01_Shared_Spine v2.3 IS.3, which reconciles the ITB record's team framing with the SE and AI&DS records' project-ownership requirement. LO2 carries both: team collaboration from the ITB record, personal responsibility for a defined contribution from the other two, which is the reconciliation the Committee settled. LO4 carries the ownership strand into reflection and is the individually attributed outcome. The prior v1.0 outcome set is retained in substance; the changes are the addition of personal responsibility to LO2 and of personal contribution to LO4. The introductory Evaluate register on LO4 is a skills performance under the B profile and does not exceed the Level 3 ladder. ## 4. Skills Programme and Delivery

### Workshop Programme

| Ref | Theme | Hours |

| W2 | From idea to plan: scoping, roles, contribution boundaries and timeline | 3 |

Workshops run as eight three-hour blocks (V10 session pattern). Facilitated activities (30 hours) are supervised team build sessions; coaching (12 hours) is per-team progress review; independent work (84 hours) is the team build plus the individual reflective piece. W2 now carries the definition of contribution boundaries, which is what makes individual attribution assessable rather than asserted.

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md v1.2). Weightings confirmed at specification sign-off; no formal examination is carried by design.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-G-Home | Team project: working artefact, build log and public demonstration; team size recorded per cohort plan (indicatively n = 4), with an individually maintained contribution log per student | Group (n = 4 indicative); take-home with supervised build sessions; graded; oral vehicle: Presentation | LO1, LO2, LO3 | 60 |
| CW-I-Home | Individual reflective account: process, teamwork, personal contribution and lessons carried forward, evidenced against the contribution log | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO2, LO4 | 40 |
| | **Total** | | | **100** |

**Attribution note.** The individually graded component is a condition of the adopted participation mode and may not be removed or converted to a group task by any carrying pathway. The contribution log is the evidence base linking the individual account to the team artefact.

Reassessment: by the failed component only, with an equivalent task, per School regulations. Where a team component is failed for reasons attributable to the team, individual reassessment is by an equivalent individual task. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level stance set at teaching-plan stage.
