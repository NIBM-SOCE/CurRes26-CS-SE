## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

### The COMMON FRAME (master-issued, reproduced VERBATIM)

> **Industrial Training (5 credits x 100 hours = 500 hours, Level 4, Profile D):** Induction and placement preparation 9; Placement (External Sourced or NIBM Breeding) 458; Supervision touchpoints 12; Reporting and assessment 21. **The placement block is scheduled outside teaching weeks** - this commitment is part of the Year 2 workload defence and may not be altered by any thread. Assessment components: placement completion, individually maintained placement log (CW), placement report (CW), and a closing **Viva-defence** (mandatory). Grading mode and weightings proposed by the thread; any pass-fail component must be justified against the TVEC competency-evidence orientation. Evidence artefacts (log, report, viva record) are auditable competency evidence; TVEC requirements are not yet final and specifications remain adaptable.

*The frame above is fixed across all four pathways; pathway flavour lives in briefs, domains and supervision, never in the frame.*

### Pathway Purpose (SE flavour: domains and supervision)

Industrial Training is the workplace-competence module of the CS(SE) pathway: a supervised industry placement in a software-engineering setting, in which students apply pathway competencies in real organisational work, operate professionally, and document the experience as auditable evidence. Placements are sourced externally or through the NIBM Breeding arrangement; oversight is by CGU with SE domain supervision. The placement block sits outside teaching weeks - the locked commitment reproduced in the frame above, part of the Year 2 workload defence (in-teaching load 1,700 notional hours, V9). The scope limit is quoted below: this module carries no taught pathway content.

> **Does NOT cover:** taught pathway content.

### Placement Arrangement (458 hours)

Placement is External Sourced or NIBM Breeding, in a software-engineering setting, scheduled as a block outside teaching weeks; this scheduling commitment is reproduced in the frame and may not be altered. Workplace supervision is agreed at placement set-up; CGU holds oversight with SE domain supervision on the academic side.

### Supervision Touchpoints (12 hours)

Scheduled touchpoints across the placement between the student, the academic supervisor and (as arranged) the workplace supervisor; the pattern (for example four 3-hour checkpoints) is set at teaching-plan stage. Touchpoint records feed the assessment evidence set.

### Reporting and Assessment (21 hours)

Log-maintenance guidance across the placement; report preparation; the closing viva.

## 5. Assessment

Components per the common frame; grading modes and weightings proposed here per the frame's rule and confirmed at sign-off.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PR-I-Class | Placement completion: sustained demonstration of workplace competence across the placement, confirmed through workplace supervision and CGU oversight | Individual; in-placement (supervised); pass-fail; threshold condition of passing the module | LO1, LO2 | 0 |
| CW-I-Home | Individually maintained placement log | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO1, LO3 | 35 |
| CW-I-Home | Placement report with the closing Viva-defence | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: Viva-defence | LO2, LO3 | 65 |

**Pass-fail justification (required by the frame):** placement completion is graded pass-fail because it is threshold competency evidence - the fact of sustained, supervised workplace performance - and TVEC workplace sign-off is by nature a threshold confirmation; percentage-grading the fact of completion would add no evidential value, while the graded log and report carry the quality differentiation. The pass-fail component carries no percentage weight and is a condition of passing the module; the graded weights sum to 100. The closing Viva-defence is attached to the report as its mandatory oral vehicle, per the taxonomy rule that oral components attach to Coursework rather than standing alone. Reassessment is by failed component; failure of placement completion is remediated by a further placement arrangement through CGU. Academic-integrity and generative-AI expectations follow School policy; the log's individual maintenance, the touchpoint records and the Viva-defence are the authorship evidence.
