## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To take communication from academic competence to workplace performance: technical reporting and documentation, persuasive proposals, interview and professional-dialogue performance, and a managed professional presence. The module is timed to precede the Industrial Training placement and is the named on-ramp to Effective Communication Skills 3 (NB5006CEM) at Level 5.

### Specification Boundary (canonical, v8.1 Phase 2)

> **Covers:** workplace and organisational communication; technical reporting and documentation; persuasive proposals and pitches; interview and professional-dialogue performance; professional presence.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** foundational communication (Effective Communication Skills I); Level 5 communication provision (NB5006CEM).
>
> *The boundary text is the audited canonical text, identical wherever carried. Any change is a change request against the Master Decisions register, not a descriptor-level edit.*

## 4. Skills Programme and Delivery

### Workshop Programme

| Ref | Theme | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Workplace communication: organisational structures, meetings and written channels | 3 |
| W2 | Technical reporting and documentation | 3 |
| W3 | Persuasive communication: proposals and pitches | 3 |
| W4 | Interview and career communication | 3 |
| W5 | Professional presence: identity, networking and conduct | 3 |
|  | Total workshop hours | 15 |

Workshops run as five three-hour blocks (V10 session pattern). Facilitated activities (21 hours) include report and proposal studios and mock-interview practice; coaching (9 hours) gives individual feedback ahead of the assessed dialogue; independent work (55 hours) completes the portfolio. Scheduling places the module before the Industrial Training placement block.

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md). Weightings confirmed at specification sign-off; no formal examination is carried by design (skills module).

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-I-Home | Professional communication portfolio: a workplace-standard technical report on the student's own pathway work, and a persuasive proposal | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO1, LO2 | 60 |
| CW-I-Class | Assessed professional dialogue: interview simulation with proposal defence and question handling | Individual; in-class; graded; oral vehicle: Viva-defence | LO2, LO3, LO4 | 40 |

Reassessment: by the failed component only, with an equivalent task, per School regulations. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level AI stance at teaching-plan stage.
