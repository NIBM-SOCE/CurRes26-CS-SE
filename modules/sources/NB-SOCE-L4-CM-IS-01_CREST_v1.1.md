## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To give every Year 2 student the practice of recognising an opportunity, shaping it into something worth pursuing, and arguing for it in front of people who can say no.

The module is deliberately about the front of the process. Students learn to see where value might be, to test whether they are right, and to state the case. It stops there. What happens after someone says yes belongs to project management and business practice, and that seam is policed in both directions.

**Workload and credit position, stated plainly.** The module is mandatory, is graded pass/fail, is a condition of award, and carries 150 engagement hours that contribute no credit. The Curriculum Committee reviewed this position on 13 August 2026 and confirmed it unchanged. The specification below is therefore held to what the module needs to work, and the scope was reduced at that review rather than extended: three strands appearing in the previous descriptor had no source in any pathway record and were withdrawn.

### Specification Boundary (quoted from 01_Shared_Spine.md v2.3, entry IS.4 - QUOTED, never edited)

> **Covers:** entrepreneurship foundations and enterprise thinking; creativity and ideation practice; innovation processes and methods; opportunity identification and evaluation; taking an idea to a stated value proposition and pitching it.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** degree-level leadership and entrepreneurial theory (NB6002CEM); project-management and business practice (Business and Project Management Practice on the SE pathway; the boundary is policed both ways); professional communication (Effective Communication Skills II).
>
> *Boundary changes are change requests against Master Decisions, not descriptor edits.*

## 4. Skills Programme and Delivery

### Workshop Programme

| Ref | Theme | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Enterprise thinking: what entrepreneurship is, and what it is not | 3 |
| W2 | Creativity and ideation practice I: generating options | 3 |
| W3 | Creativity and ideation practice II: shaping and selecting | 3 |
| W4 | Innovation processes and methods | 3 |
| W5 | Opportunity identification: where value might be | 3 |
| W6 | Opportunity evaluation: testing whether the opportunity is real | 3 |
| W7 | From opportunity to value proposition | 3 |
| W8 | Pitching: making the case and answering the challenge | 3 |
|  | Total workshop hours | 24 |

Workshops run as eight three-hour blocks (V10 session pattern). Facilitated activities (30 hours) develop the team opportunity concept; coaching (12 hours) is per-team mentoring; independent work (84 hours) completes the concept pack and individual reflection. Engagement hours follow the B-3 pattern in full; per V9 they are not counted toward award notional totals.

**Adoption record for this programme.** Against the prior v1.0 structure, the two business-model workshops and the intellectual-property workshop are withdrawn, and the two prototyping-and-validation workshops are replaced by W5 and W6, which treat opportunity identification and evaluation as the adopted text specifies. Total workshop hours are unchanged at 24. The module now spends its time on what its boundary names.

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md v1.2). Grading is pass/fail on every component (V9).

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-G-Home | Opportunity concept pack: a framed opportunity, the evidence that it is real, a stated value proposition, closed by a team pitch with question handling; individually maintained contribution log per student | Group (n = 4 indicative); take-home with facilitated sessions; pass/fail; oral vehicle: Presentation | LO1, LO2, LO3 | Pass/fail |
| CW-I-Home | Individual reflective statement on innovation practice and personal contribution | Individual; take-home; pass/fail; oral vehicle: None | LO4 | Pass/fail |

Both components must be passed; there is no percentage weighting (V9; taxonomy Grading = pass/fail). Reassessment is by resubmission of the failed component with an equivalent task. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level stance set at teaching-plan stage.
