## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To equip a student exiting at Diploma level with a clear, realistic view of the computing employment landscape and an initial evidence portfolio to enter it. The module maps sector roles, routes and entry expectations against the student's pathway, and turns completed Year 1 work into structured, auditable evidence of capability. It is a core competency-evidence module of the Diploma exit and is oriented to the NVQ Level 5 target.

### Specification Boundary (quoted from NIBM_SOCE_CMN_ExitBlock_ModuleRecords v1.2 - QUOTED, never edited)

> **Covers:** the employment landscape of the computing and IT sector; industry roles, career routes and entry expectations; sector awareness aligned to the student's pathway; portfolio seeding.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** career strategy and professional-profile depth (Professional Standing and Career Strategy, HND exit); workplace conduct foundations (Professional Practice Foundations).
>
> *Boundary changes are change requests against Master Decisions, not descriptor edits.*

### Exit-Award Orientation (D9; TVEC)

> This is a core competency-evidence module of the Diploma exit award, oriented to the NVQ Level 5 target. Independent-work hours are directed to the evidence portfolio (skills inventory mapped to completed Year 1 artefacts) and a personal entry-route plan; both structured as auditable competency evidence against the NVQ Level 5 target. **TVEC accreditation is an active pursuit and is not yet granted; additional requirements may be introduced, and the competency-evidence mapping above remains adaptable to confirmed requirements.**

## 4. Skills Programme and Delivery

### Workshop Programme

| Ref | Theme | Hours |

| W1 | The computing and IT sector: structure, roles and routes | 3 |

Workshops run as five three-hour blocks (V10 session pattern). Facilitated activities (21 hours) build the evidence artefacts; coaching (9 hours) gives individual feedback on developing portfolio pieces; independent work (55 hours) completes the competency-evidence portfolio. **Note:** the B-2 hour split is 15/21/9/55 per 05_Hours_Model.md v1.2, which supersedes the 16/20/8/56 indicative split in the central record (pre-V10).

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md); weightings confirmed at specification sign-off. No formal examination is carried by design (skills module). Assessment artefacts are structured as auditable competency evidence for the exit award.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-I-Home | Evidence portfolio: a structured inventory of skills mapped to completed Year 1 artefacts, with a personal entry-route plan | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO1, LO2, LO3 | 60 |
| CW-I-Class | Portfolio presentation: the student presents their evidence portfolio and entry route to a panel with question handling | Individual; in-class; graded; oral vehicle: Presentation | LO1, LO3 | 40 |

Reassessment: by the failed component only, with an equivalent task, per School regulations. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level AI stance at teaching-plan stage.
