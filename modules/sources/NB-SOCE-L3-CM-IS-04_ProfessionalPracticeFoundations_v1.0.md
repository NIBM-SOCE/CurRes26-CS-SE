## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To prepare a Diploma-exiting student for the workplace: expected professional conduct and communication, honest and effective application materials, and a clear grasp of an entry-level computing professional's responsibilities. The module is practical and evidence-producing, and is a core competency-evidence module of the Diploma exit oriented to the NVQ Level 5 target.

### Specification Boundary (canonical, v8.1 Phase 2)

> **Covers:** workplace conduct and professional expectations; CV and application craft; interview and workplace communication basics; introductory professional responsibility.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** ethics depth and professional-standing strategy (HND exit block); communication-skills breadth (Effective Communication Skills I and II).
>
> *The boundary text is the audited canonical text, identical wherever carried. Any change is a change request against the Master Decisions register, not a descriptor-level edit.*

### Exit-Award Orientation (D9; TVEC)

> This is a core competency-evidence module of the Diploma exit award, oriented to the NVQ Level 5 target. Independent-work hours are directed to a portfolio of application materials (CV, professional profile) and a workplace-conduct case response, mapped to workplace-readiness competencies against the NVQ Level 5 target. **TVEC accreditation is an active pursuit and is not yet granted; additional requirements may be introduced, and the competency-evidence mapping above remains adaptable to confirmed requirements.**

Facilitated activities (21 hours) build the evidence artefacts; coaching (9 hours) gives individual feedback on developing portfolio pieces; independent work (55 hours) completes the competency-evidence portfolio. **Note:** the B-2 hour split is 15/21/9/55 per 05_Hours_Model.md v1.2, which supersedes the 16/20/8/56 indicative split in the central record (pre-V10).

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md); weightings confirmed at specification sign-off. No formal examination is carried by design (skills module). Assessment artefacts are structured as auditable competency evidence for the exit award.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-I-Home | Application portfolio: CV, professional profile and a written workplace-conduct case response | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO2, LO3 | 60 |
| CW-I-Class | Practical professional scenario: a conduct-and-communication simulation with reflection | Individual; in-class; graded; oral vehicle: Presentation | LO1, LO3 | 40 |

Reassessment: by the failed component only, with an equivalent task, per School regulations. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level AI stance at teaching-plan stage.
