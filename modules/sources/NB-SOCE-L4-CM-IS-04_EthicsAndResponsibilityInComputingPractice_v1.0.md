## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To ensure a student exiting at Higher National Diploma level leaves with a working command of professional ethics and responsibility in computing practice. The module exists precisely because exiting students never reach the degree-level treatment of this territory: it develops ethical case analysis, the practitioner's responsibilities under data-protection and privacy obligations in the Sri Lankan and international context, and reflection grounded in placement experience. It is a core competency-evidence module of the HND exit oriented to the NVQ Level 6 target.

### Specification Boundary (canonical, v8.1 Phase 2)

> **Covers:** professional ethics in computing practice; responsibility, accountability and duty of care; data protection and privacy obligations in the Sri Lankan and international context; ethical case analysis grounded in placement experience.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** degree-level social, legal and ethical breadth (the Level 5 provision for continuing students); security-governance depth (pathway modules where carried).
>
> *The boundary text is the audited canonical text, identical wherever carried. Any change is a change request against the Master Decisions register, not a descriptor-level edit.*

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md); weightings confirmed at specification sign-off. No formal examination is carried by design (skills module). Assessment artefacts are structured as auditable competency evidence for the exit award.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-I-Home | Ethics case portfolio: analysed cases using recognised frameworks, including a data-protection and privacy responsibility analysis grounded in placement | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO1, LO2 | 60 |
| CW-I-Class | Structured ethical discussion: an assessed reasoned discussion of an ethical case, with an individual reflective position | Individual; in-class; graded; oral vehicle: Viva-defence | LO1, LO3 | 40 |

Reassessment: by the failed component only, with an equivalent task, per School regulations. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level AI stance at teaching-plan stage.
