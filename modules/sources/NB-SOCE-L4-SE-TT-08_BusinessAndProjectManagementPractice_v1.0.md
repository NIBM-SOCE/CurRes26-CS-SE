## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

The module builds the business and project-management practice of the CS(SE) vertical: how software projects are initiated, planned, estimated and tracked in both agile and plan-driven contexts, and how software work sits inside a business and organisational context. It is the thirteenth SE Year 2 module, and the descriptor restates its admission case as the register requires.

**D1 justification restatement (issues-register-backed, per the descriptor obligation):** the admission has two legs. (1) The consultation register: I-013 (Herath - project-management skills in the core curriculum, needed for SE; the v8 answer was a taught component inside the project module, now upgraded to a dedicated module) and I-014 (Mahawatta - a dedicated PM module for HDSE; the DevOps half of I-014 is met in Web and Cloud Application Development and Enterprise Application Development). (2) The credit-bearing business gap: V9 removed CREST's credit weight, leaving the HND profile with no credit-bearing business content; this module restores 2 credits of it, and unlike a pure employability admission it carries a feeder claim into the Level 6 management-practice destination. **Watch condition: this justification is restated at mid-point review.** The scope limits are quoted below - entrepreneurship stays with CREST, communication skill with its own module, business-information-systems depth with ITB, and DevOps tooling with its two homes.

### Specification Boundary (from the record set - QUOTED, never edited)

> **Covers:** project management practice (initiation, planning, estimation, tracking, agile and plan-driven contexts); business and organisational context of software work; introductory business processes; professional practice.

## 4. Curriculum and Delivery

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy; weightings confirmed at specification sign-off.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-I-Home | Project plan and business-context analysis: a full plan (scope, schedule, estimates, tracking approach) for a supplied software-project case, with an analysis of its business and organisational context | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: Presentation | LO1, LO2, LO3 | 60 |
| CW-I-Class | Supervised planning exercise under open-resource conditions: timed estimation and tracking tasks on a fresh case | Individual; in-class; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO1, LO2 | 40 |

No examination is carried by design: the assessed capability is planning and analysis practice on realistic cases, and the supervised in-class exercise on a fresh case is the integrity anchor for individual capability. Weights sum to 100. Reassessment is by failed component. Academic-integrity and generative-AI expectations follow School policy.
