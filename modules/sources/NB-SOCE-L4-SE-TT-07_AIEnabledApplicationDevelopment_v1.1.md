## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

The module positions CS(SE) graduates for AI-augmented software roles: integrating AI and machine-learning capabilities into applications by consuming services and APIs, embedding pre-built models, designing application features around probabilistic outputs, and practising AI-assisted development responsibly. It sits firmly on the use side of the use-not-build axis.

**D1 exception restatement (required by the register, per the descriptor obligation):** this module is **use-not-build** - no model building, no model training, no machine-learning theory; the depth owner of model building and training is the AI&DS flagship module, Applied Artificial Intelligence (V6 retitle), and the hard boundary quoted below is what prevented duplication at the cross-pathway duplicate-content check. The module carries no hard Coventry feeder and is a documented employability exception admitted on governance and branding grounds. **Its watch condition is restated verbatim in intent: it is first in line for consolidation or removal at external review**, and the shared-staffing delivery risk with AI&DS is named under staffing. The descriptor holds the scope discipline that keeps the exception defensible: every unit below is application-side.

### Specification Boundary (from the record set - QUOTED, never edited)

> **Covers:** integrating AI and machine-learning capabilities into applications; consuming AI/ML services and APIs; embedding pre-built models; AI-assisted development practice. This is AI use, on the use-not-build axis.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** building, training or theorising machine-learning models (the AI&DS pathway's territory - the hard boundary that prevents duplication in the Phase 2 duplicate-content check; cross-check against the Applied Artificial Intelligence retitle, V6).
>
> *Boundary changes are change requests against Master Decisions, not descriptor edits.*

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy; weightings confirmed at specification sign-off.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-I-Home | AI-enabled application build: an application integrating at least one AI/ML service and one embedded pre-built model, with a responsible-use statement covering AI-assisted development in its own construction | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: Viva-defence | LO1, LO2, LO3 | 65 |
| CW-I-Class | Supervised applied exercise under open-resource conditions: service-integration and output-handling tasks | Individual; in-class; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO1 | 35 |

No examination is carried by design: the assessed capability is applied integration, and in a module that teaches AI-assisted development the Viva-defence is the deliberate integrity instrument - students defend what they built and how assistance was used, which the responsible-use statement documents. Weights sum to 100. Reassessment is by failed component. Academic-integrity and generative-AI expectations follow School policy, with this module's own U5/U6 practice as the taught foundation for them.
