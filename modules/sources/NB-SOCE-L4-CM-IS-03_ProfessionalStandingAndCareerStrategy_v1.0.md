## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To help a student exiting at Higher National Diploma level convert two years of study and a placement into a deliberate career strategy: a professional profile with standing, a progression plan, a continuing professional development plan, and a working knowledge of the professional-body and certification landscape for their pathway. It builds on the Industrial Training experience and is a core competency-evidence module of the HND exit oriented to the NVQ Level 6 target.

### Specification Boundary (canonical, v8.1 Phase 2)

> **Covers:** career strategy and progression planning; professional profile and standing; CPD planning; professional-body and certification landscape for the pathway.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** ethics and responsibility depth (Ethics and Responsibility in Computing Practice); sector-landscape introduction (Industry Horizons).
>
> *The boundary text is the audited canonical text, identical wherever carried. Any change is a change request against the Master Decisions register, not a descriptor-level edit.*

### Exit-Award Orientation (D9; TVEC)

> This is a core competency-evidence module of the Higher National Diploma exit award, oriented to the NVQ Level 6 target. Independent-work hours are directed to a professional profile, a career and progression strategy grounded in placement experience, and a CPD plan mapped to the pathway's certification landscape; structured as auditable competency evidence against the NVQ Level 6 target. **TVEC accreditation is an active pursuit and is not yet granted; additional requirements may be introduced, and the competency-evidence mapping above remains adaptable to confirmed requirements.**

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md); weightings confirmed at specification sign-off. No formal examination is carried by design (skills module). Assessment artefacts are structured as auditable competency evidence for the exit award.

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-I-Home | Career strategy portfolio: professional profile, progression strategy informed by placement, and a CPD plan against the pathway certification landscape | Individual; take-home; graded; oral vehicle: None | LO1, LO2, LO3 | 60 |
| CW-I-Class | Strategy defence: the student presents and defends their career strategy and CPD plan to a panel | Individual; in-class; graded; oral vehicle: Viva-defence | LO2, LO3 | 40 |

Reassessment: by the failed component only, with an equivalent task, per School regulations. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level AI stance at teaching-plan stage.
