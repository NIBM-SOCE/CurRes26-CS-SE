## 2. Module Purpose and Aims

To form the entrepreneurial and innovation capability the programme has always carried: recognising opportunities, shaping value propositions and business models, validating ideas, pitching ventures, and practising responsible innovation. The module is delivered in full as a mandatory component and assessed pass/fail without credit weighting: a deliberate compliance decision that preserves the formation while keeping the credit-bearing load defensible.

### Specification Boundary (canonical, v8.1 Phase 2)

> **Covers:** opportunity recognition and creative ideation; value propositions and business-model fundamentals; validation and innovation practice; venture pitching; intellectual-property and responsible-innovation awareness.
>
> **Does NOT cover:** the leadership and entrepreneurial practice content of the degree years (NB6002CEM, where carried); pathway business modules (ITB owners named in the ITB records).
>
> *The boundary text is the audited canonical text, identical wherever carried. Any change is a change request against the Master Decisions register, not a descriptor-level edit.*

### Condition-of-Award Framing (V9; SLQF-06 defence)

> CREST is retained in full as a mandatory Year 2 component and a **condition of the award**: completion (pass in both components) is required for Year 2 completion and for the HND exit award. It carries no credit weighting; its engagement hours follow the B-3 delivery pattern but are not counted toward award notional totals. This is the deliberate compliance decision of V9, defended under SLQF-06 as a condition-of-award competency component rather than a credit-bearing unit, and it keeps the Year 2 credit-bearing load within the defensible envelope while preserving the entrepreneurship formation in full.

## 5. Assessment

Components per the Assessment Component Taxonomy (04_Conventions.md). Grading is Pass-Fail on every component (V9).

| Component code | Task | Attributes | ILOs | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CW-G-Home | Venture concept pack: framed opportunity, value proposition, outline business model and validation evidence, closed by a team pitch; individually maintained contribution log per student | Group (n = 4 indicative); take-home with facilitated sessions; PASS-FAIL; oral vehicle: Presentation | LO1, LO2, LO3 | Pass-Fail |
| CW-I-Home | Individual reflective statement on innovation practice, contribution and responsible-innovation considerations | Individual; take-home; PASS-FAIL; oral vehicle: None | LO4 | Pass-Fail |

Both components must be passed; there is no percentage weighting (V9; taxonomy Grading = Pass-Fail). Reassessment is by resubmission of the failed component with an equivalent task. All assessment under the academic integrity policy including its generative-AI provisions; module-level AI stance at teaching-plan stage.
